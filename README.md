
# My Favorite Movie

## Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse

**Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** is one of my favorite animated movies because of its unique animation, interesting story, and inspiring characters.

### Why I Like This Movie
- The animation style is creative.
- The story is exciting and meaningful.
- The characters have different personalities.
- It teaches us to believe in ourselves.

### My Favorite Character

**Miles Morales** is my favorite character because he learns how to become a hero and accept his responsibilities.

### My Favorite Quote

> Anyone can wear the mask.
